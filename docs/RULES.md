## Locate a finding without guessing a repair

Text lists handle, zero-based segment index, layer and endpoint/contact position.
The first segment of LINE is index 0. Polyline indices follow its stored vertex
order, including the closing edge when present. Keep the exact export: an edited
copy may have different handles or coordinates. Crossings show approximate
display locations; classification is on the original parsed float values.
Locate the geometry in CAD and review operation intent. Coincidence may be an
intentional cut/etch overlap; no deletion or automatic closure is advised.
